Thank you for choosing Express Music Player ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may access, how that information is used, and how we protect your privacy.


Express Music Player is primarily designed as an offline music player. Our goal is to provide a simple and private music-listening experience without requiring users to create an account, provide personal information, or have an internet connection for its core music playback features.

1. Information We Collect

Express Music Player does not require you to create an account or log in to use its core features.

We do not collect personal information through the App's core functionality such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal documents
  • Payment card information

2. Music and Media Files

Express Music Player is designed to play music and supported media files stored locally on your device.

When you grant the required media or storage permission, the App may access supported audio files to:

  • Find available music
  • Display music information
  • Play audio files
  • Create and manage playlists or playback queues
  • Display album artwork where supported
  • Provide music-management features

Your music files remain on your device. Express Music Player does not upload your music files to our servers or transmit your personal media files to us.

3. Storage and Media Permissions

Depending on your Android version, Express Music Player may request permission to access music and media files stored on your device. This permission is required for the App to scan, identify, organize, and play supported music files.

The App uses this permission only to provide its music-player and media-management features. Your music and media files remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers or transmitted to us through the App's core offline functionality.

You can manage or revoke applicable permissions through your device's system settings. Please note that denying the required permission may prevent the App from finding or playing music stored on your device.

4. Audio and Media Access

Express Music Player may access information associated with locally stored media files, such as:

  • Song title
  • Artist name
  • Album name
  • Duration
  • Album artwork, where available
  • File location or URI required for playback
  • Other standard media metadata

This information is used to provide music playback and library-management functionality and does not need to be uploaded to an external server for the core offline functionality.

5. Notification Permission

On Android versions where notification permission is required, Express Music Player may request permission to display notifications.

Notifications may be used for:

  • Music playback controls
  • Showing the currently playing song
  • Playback-related information
  • Important application information

You can disable notification permission through your device's system settings.

6. Device Information

Express Music Player's core offline functionality does not require the collection of personally identifiable device information.

The Android operating system may provide certain technical information to the App when necessary for normal operation, compatibility, permissions, audio playback, or error handling. This may include Android version, device compatibility information, App version, screen or system configuration, and media framework information.

We do not use this information to identify you personally.

7. Internet Connection

Express Music Player is designed primarily as an offline music player. The core music playback functionality does not require an internet connection.

If future versions introduce features that require internet access, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

8. Personal Information

We do not intentionally request or collect personal information through the App's core offline functionality.

If you voluntarily contact us through email or another communication method, we may receive the information that you choose to provide. This information will be used to respond to your request, provide support, or address your feedback.

9. How We Use Information

Any information accessed by Express Music Player is used only for legitimate application functionality, including:

  • Providing music playback
  • Managing your local music library
  • Reading supported media metadata
  • Displaying album artwork
  • Maintaining playback functionality
  • Providing notifications
  • Improving application compatibility
  • Diagnosing technical problems when applicable
  • Responding to user feedback or support requests

10. Data Sharing

We respect your privacy. Express Music Player does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your locally stored music files and media information are not intentionally shared with third parties through the App's core offline functionality.

11. Third-Party Services

The current core version of Express Music Player is designed to operate as an offline music player.

We do not claim that any third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud-storage service is used unless such a service is actually integrated into the App.

12. Advertising

The core version of Express Music Player is designed without requiring advertisements for its offline music-player functionality.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising and data practices.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the privacy of users. Because Express Music Player is primarily an offline application, much of the information used by the App remains directly on the user's device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also maintain appropriate security on their devices.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Express Music Player does not maintain a central account containing your personal information.

Music files and other locally stored application data remain on your device according to your device's storage and Android application settings.

You can remove locally stored application data by:

  • Deleting individual files through your device
  • Removing playlists or application data where applicable
  • Clearing the App's data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling the App

Once locally stored application data is deleted, it may not be recoverable through the App.

15. Children's Privacy

Express Music Player is designed as a general-purpose music-player application. The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core offline music-player features..

The App does not collect personal information from children or other users through its core offline functionality. Music and media information accessed by the App remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to us.

We are committed to providing a privacy-respecting experience for all users.

16. Your Choices and Permissions

You remain in control of the permissions granted to Express Music Player. Depending on your Android version, you can review or change permissions through:

Settings → Apps → Express Music Player → Permissions

You may deny or revoke permissions where Android allows it. Please note that certain permissions are necessary for specific features.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, new features, Android requirements, data practices, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
