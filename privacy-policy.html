Express Music Player is designed as an offline music player. We do not require an account to use the app's core offline features.

Information We Collect

The app is designed not to collect or transmit personal information to our servers as part of its core offline functionality.

The app may access audio/media files on your device when you grant the relevant permission. These files are used locally for features such as playback and music management.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties through the app's core offline functionality.

Permissions

Media/audio access may be required so the app can find and play music stored on your device. Permissions are used only for the corresponding app functionality.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
