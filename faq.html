How does Express Music Player work?

The app plays audio files available on your device and provides local playback and music-management features.

Does the app require an account?

No account is required for the offline music-player features.

Where are my music files stored?

Your music files remain on your device. The app accesses supported media files according to the permissions granted by you.

Does the app stream music online?

No. Express Music Player is designed as an offline music player.
